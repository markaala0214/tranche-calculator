📐 How the 1st Tranche Fund is Calculated

Mode: With School Furniture (WITH_SF)
  • School Furniture: # of CL × ₱154,000
  • RTA less SF: RTA Amount - School Furniture
  • EAO (LGU): (RTA Amount - School Furniture) × 0.25%
  • Total Fund Allocation: RTA Amount + EAO (LGU)
  • 🎯 1st Tranche Fund Allocation:
    (50% × RTA Amount) + EAO (LGU)
Mode: Without School Furniture (WITHOUT_SF)
  • School Furniture: ₱0.00
  • EAO (LGU): RTA Amount × 0.25%
  • Total Fund Allocation: RTA Amount + EAO (LGU)
  • 🎯 1st Tranche Fund Allocation:
    (50% × RTA Amount) + EAO (LGU)
* Validation Condition: The calculation is considered VALID / PASS if Total Fund Allocation ≤ Total ABC Amount.
